Artisan AI
AI-Powered Product Intelligence and Marketing Assistant for Artisans

Artisan AI is an AI-powered platform designed to help local artisans and small-scale vendors transform their handmade products into market-ready offerings. A vendor can provide an image of their product, and Artisan AI analyzes it to generate useful business insights, including product identification, categorization, target customers, unique selling points, potential markets, suitable occasions, production cost insights, and marketing strategies.

The Problem :-

Many artisans create high-quality handmade products but face challenges beyond production, such as limited knowledge of digital marketing, difficulty identifying target customers, lack of access to market research, difficulty positioning products effectively, limited branding and marketing expertise, and challenges in identifying suitable markets and occasions. As a result, valuable handmade products may fail to reach the customers who are most likely to purchase them.

Our Solution :-

Artisan AI acts as an AI-powered business and marketing assistant for artisans. Instead of requiring vendors to have expertise in marketing, branding, or market research, the platform analyzes their product and provides actionable insights in a simple and accessible format.

The workflow is simple: the vendor provides a product image, Artisan AI analyzes the product, and the system generates information such as product name, production cost insights, product category, target customers, unique selling points, potential markets, suitable occasions and seasons, and customized marketing strategies.

Key Features:-
AI Product Analysis

The vendor provides an image of their handmade product, and Artisan AI analyzes its visual characteristics to generate relevant product information.

Product Identification

The system suggests an appropriate product name and generates relevant product information based on the uploaded image.

Product Categorization

Artisan AI identifies the most relevant product category to help vendors organize and position their products.

Production Cost Insights

The platform can work with production cost information provided by the vendor and use it as part of the overall business analysis.

Target Customer Identification

Artisan AI identifies potential customer segments that are likely to be interested in the product.

Unique Selling Points

The system identifies characteristics that can differentiate the product from competing products and converts them into potential unique selling points.

Potential Market Identification

The platform suggests potential markets and customer segments where the product may have stronger demand.

Occasion and Seasonal Opportunities

Artisan AI identifies festivals, occasions, seasons, and other situations where the product may have increased market potential.

AI-Powered Marketing Strategies

The system generates practical marketing strategies and ideas tailored to the product.

Why Artisan AI?

Traditional business and marketing tools are often designed for established businesses with access to market research, branding teams, and digital marketing expertise. Artisan AI aims to make these capabilities accessible to individual artisans and small vendors.

The platform helps vendors understand what they are selling, who they should sell it to, where they can sell it, why customers should choose it, and how they can market it.

Getting Started
Prerequisites

Make sure you have Node.js, Git, and a supported AI API key installed or available.

Clone the Repository
git clone YOUR_GITHUB_REPOSITORY_URL
cd artisan
Install Backend Dependencies
cd backend
npm install
Configure Environment Variables

Create a .env file inside the backend directory and add your API credentials.

YOUR_AI_API_KEY=your_api_key_here

Do not commit your .env file or API keys to GitHub.

Start the Backend
node server.js

The backend will start on the configured local port.

Run the Frontend

Open the frontend in your browser or use a local development server according to the project configuration.

Security

Sensitive credentials are stored using environment variables and are excluded from the public repository. The .env file, node_modules, API keys, and other secret credentials should never be committed to GitHub.
